CLEAR ANSWERS

Common Questions

Clear answers regarding our exterior wash process, safety, and rates.

How much does pressure washing cost? +

Pricing depends on square footage, material type, and the level of staining. Most residential driveways start around $120–$180, while whole-house soft washes range from $220–$450. We provide 100% free, no-obligation fixed quotes upfront.

Is pressure washing safe for my siding and roof? +

Yes! For delicate surfaces like vinyl siding, stucco, and shingles, we utilize specialized low-pressure "Soft Wash" technology combined with eco-friendly cleaning detergents, eliminating moss and algae without forcing water behind walls.

How long does a typical wash take? +

A standard two-car driveway takes roughly 1.5 to 2 hours. Full home siding washes typically require 2 to 3.5 hours. Commercial projects vary based on square footage.

Do I need to be home during service? +

No, you do not need to be home as long as exterior water spigots are accessible, windows are securely latched, and gates are unlocked for technician access.

How often should I pressure wash? +

Most local homeowners wash driveways and house siding once every 12 to 18 months to prevent permanent stain etching and keep algae spores from damaging finishes.

Are you licensed and insured? +

Absolutely. Mr. Bee's carries $2,000,000 comprehensive commercial general liability insurance and worker compensation protection for absolute peace of mind.
