## Brand & Style

This design system blends high-performance commercial credibility with the punchy, energetic personality of an elite exterior cleaning brand. It speaks to property owners, facility directors, and homeowners who demand immaculate curb appeal, rapid turnarounds, and undeniable proof of quality.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Corporate** reliability with **High-Contrast Tactical Utility**:
- **Trust & Precision:** Deep marine navy foundations signal institutional stability, licensed craftsmanship, and structural authority.
- **Power & Cleanliness:** Vivid aqua and water-blue tones channel high-velocity hydrostatic pressure, clarity, and refreshed surfaces.
- **Action & Conversion:** High-voltage bee yellow is deployed exclusively for decisive interactions (instant quotes, emergency call routing, booking confirmations).
- **Tactile Polish:** Ultra-crisp iconography, subtle hexagonal grid watermarks, and smooth elevated card planes make complex commercial offerings instantly digestible and reassuring.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate hierarchy between brand trust, pristine water dynamics, and conversion-driving triggers.

- **Primary (`#0F2537`):** Command Navy. Anchors top navigation, hero backgrounds, primary text headlines, and authoritative structural framing.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`):** High-Pressure Aqua. Highlights interactive features, icon glyphs, badge indicators, before/after slider trackers, and secondary links. Pair with a lighter tint (`#E0F2FE`) for soft surface container fills.
- **Tertiary (`#FFC400`):** Electric Sting Yellow. Reserved strictly for primary action drivers: "Get Instant Quote", "Call Now", booking steps, and critical promotional ribbons. Text sitting directly on this yellow accent must use Navy (`#0B1B2B`) for crisp AA/AAA contrast.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Dark Slate Neutral (`#0B1B2B`): Core typography and darkest container fills.
  - Mid Grays (`#64748B`, `#94A3B8`): Subtext, meta labels, and structural dividers.
  - Light Grounding (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`): Main page backgrounds and alternating section surface layers.
  - Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`): Main cards, elevated modules, and modal containers.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commanding, high-impact titles with **Inter** for dense service specs, customer quotes, and step-by-step estimators.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with extra-bold weight (700–800) and tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a solid, punchy commercial presence.
- **Body:** `Inter` handles operational details, warranty clauses, and address data with open letter spacing and clear optical distinction.
- **Labels & Badges:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` in semi-bold and bold weights reinforces high visibility on quote CTA badges, phone trigger buttons, and trust stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a structured **12-column responsive fluid grid** with generous safe margins to preserve clarity across high-conversion funnels.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max canvas width 1280px with 32px safe margins.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px canvas margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px canvas margins.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an 8pt modular scale. Form inputs, booking cards, and comparison matrices maintain dense `space-sm` to `space-md` gaps internally, while macro page sections breathe with `space-xl` and outer margins.
- **Reflow Behavior:** 3-column service cards (Roof Wash, Driveway, Commercial Fleet) collapse smoothly into a 2-column grid on tablet and single-column stack on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean tonal surfaces paired with refined multi-layered drop shadows tinted with the primary navy:

- **Base Layer (Flat):** Off-white canvas `#F8FAFC` layered against section-level `#F1F5F9` panels.
- **Low Elevation (Resting Cards & Service Tiles):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a crisp sub-pixel border (`1px solid rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.08)`) and soft ambient lift:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.03);`
- **Mid Elevation (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Enhanced lift to invite engagement:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(2, 132, 197, 0.08);`
- **High Elevation (Quote Modals & Sticky CTA Bars):** 
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(11, 27, 43, 0.18), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 27, 43, 0.12);`
- **Surface Texture:** Subtle hexagonal honeycomb vectors overlaid at 3% opacity (`#0284C7`) inside Navy hero headers and footer bands to celebrate the brand motif without compromising legibility.

## Shapes

The design system embraces balanced, accessible geometry with a base roundedness factor of **2** (`0.5rem` / `8px`):

- **Input Fields & Micro Elements:** Standard `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` equivalent) to guarantee crisp form alignments.
- **Cards & Service Modules:** Elevated to `1rem` (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`) to feel friendly, modern, and clean.
- **Hero Windows & Testimonial Panels:** Scaled up to `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) for rich visual framing.
- **Conversion Badges & Quick Action Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) for tap targets, commercial guarantee badges, and instant call actions.

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion CTA (Sting Yellow):** High-visibility background `#FFC400`, text `#0B1B2B`, font weight 700, padding `14px 28px`, border radius `0.75rem`. - **Secondary Action (Water Blue):** Background `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. Hover shifts to `#0369A1`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Border `2px solid #0F2537`, transparent fill, text `#0F2537`. Hover fills with `#0F2537` and text `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Trust Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Small capsule indicators (`rounded-full`) with `#E0F2FE` background and `#0369A1` text, flanked by micro checkmark or water-droplet icons.
- **Rating / Review Chips:** `#FEF3C7` fill with dark amber text and `#FFC400` stars.

### Input Fields & Instant Quote Estimators
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F2537`, padding `14px 16px`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#0284C7` accompanied by a `3px` focus ring `rgba(2, 132, 197, 0.20)`. No default black browser outlines.
- **Floating Labels:** Crisp 12px uppercase labels in `#64748B`.

### Service Cards & Before/After Modules
- **Service Cards:** White base `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, padding `24px`, corner radius `1rem`. Houses a vibrant blue icon badge (`48x48px` pill with `#E0F2FE` fill and `#0284C7` glyph).
- **Comparison Sliders:** Split image component featuring a central vertical slider line (`#FFC400`) with circular dual-arrow handle for showcasing pressure-washing results.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20px squares, radius 4px, border `2px solid #94A3B8`. Checked state fills `#0284C7` with white check vector.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular selection rings that trigger a solid `#0284C7` dot fill on active choice with slight scale animation.

### Sticky Direct-Booking Bar (Mobile-Optimized)
- Fixed bottom container for mobile viewports using frosted `#0F2537` background with 90% opacity, backdrop blur 12px, offering a single-tap "Call for Fast Quote" phone CTA in `#FFC400`.
